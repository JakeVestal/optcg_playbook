Bonney Playbook

OP17 meta · October 2026 · Focus: rest / freeze key bodies so Hody or a 9-cost can close

→ Green Utility Cards (Rest / Freeze / DON / Draw / Keywords)

Core rule: Rest the body that is about to swing (not the one that already attacked). Kill the one that is already rested. Drop Hody on 7 or a 9-cost on 9 the turn you can, even if the board is imperfect. One free 10k–12k swing is usually the game.
Swing math (practical heuristic):
Most meta decks run roughly 8–12 cards with +2000 counter (plus some +1000s and events). Average card in hand is ~900–1000 counter power. Against a typical 5-card hand with no DON!! up, assume ~4–5k of counter available.
For a 12k body: swing at least 14k–16k to force real counter investment or have a good chance of going through. Higher if they have DON!! active (each standing DON!! roughly doubles the threat of an Event counter). Ties go to the attacker.
Leader vs Character (when to go face):
Swing the leader when you can take a life and the remaining board cannot kill you next turn, or when the character you would clear is already frozen and cannot attack.
Swing the character when it is about to attack, lock one of your bodies, or is the one that makes face swings fail (Newgate, Shiki, Beckman).
If the board can still swing back and finish you, clear or freeze the attacker first. A failed face swing only feeds counters and leaves the body that ends the game. (Seen in both the Rosinante and OP17 Shanks games.)

Black Imu

OP13-079 Imu
OP13-082 Five Elders
OP13-084 Ju Peter
OP13-091 Mars

Win condition: Flood with Celestial Dragons, then OP13-082 trashes the board and replays up to five 5000-power Elders from trash. Once trash ≥7 most Elders cannot be removed by effects.

Effect KO: No. Laboon not required.

Key turn: OP13-082 hits (usually at 10 DON!!). Earliest realistic is their 5th turn going second under perfect conditions.

Disrupt: Rest active Elders before they swing. Freeze the biggest one with Carrot. Resting OP13-082 does not stop its Activate: Main.

Your plan: Race before the Elders come down. Take early life so you can drop Hody on 7 or a 9-cost on 9 and start clearing the rested ones.

Priority targets (once Elders are out)

  1. St. Shepherd Ju Peter (OP13-084) — sets all Five Elders to 7000 base power
  2. St. Marcus Mars (OP13-091) — On Play KO (cost ≤5) + becomes Blocker
  3. Any active Elder about to swing this turn
  4. St. Ethanbaron V. Nusjuro (attack / power reduction)
  5. St. Jaygarcia Saturn / St. Topman Warcury (searcher / defensive)
Swing note: Elders are usually 5k–7k base. A 9-cost Shanks or Hody (with a little DON!!) is often enough to force counters or trade. Against a 12k+ body, push to 14k+.
No green card can rest or destroy The Empty Throne stage. Ignore it and play around the bodies.

Nasty tricks: Starts with The Empty Throne (OP13-099) free. Plays Five Elders for 3 DON!! via the Stage. OP13-082 trashes the whole board and plays up to 5 different Elders from trash. No Events ≥2 cost. Elders become hard to remove once trash is high.

Purple Enel

OP15-058 Enel
6-cost Enel

Win condition: 6-DON!! deck. Leader dumps 5 DON!! at once and attaches 4 rested DON!! to a cheap body for 10k–14k swings. Closes with sticky 6-cost Enel (immune +2000 while ≤6 DON!!).

Effect KO: No. Laboon not required.

Key turn: Leader ability goes off and a 1-cost (Ohm/Shura) or the 6-cost Enel receives the 4 DON!!.

Disrupt: Rest the body that just received the DON!!. Freeze the 6-cost Enel. Clear the pumped body the same turn.

Your plan: Do not leave a 10k+ attacker active. Take life early and force them to spend the dump defensively.

Priority targets

  • The Character that just received the 4 DON!!
  • OP15-060 / OP15-118 Enel
  • Ohm, Shura
Swing note: The pumped body is often 10k–14k. Rest it first, then swing with a closer (Hody or 9-cost) that can push past 14k. The 6-cost Enel is sticky but still dies to a rested trade if you freeze or rest it.

Nasty tricks: DON!! deck is only 6 cards. Leader adds 1 active + 4 rested and attaches the 4 to one Character for massive swings. 6-cost Enel is sticky.

Rocks D. Xebec

OP17-039 Rocks
OP17-118 Rocks
OP17-048 Shiki
OP17-049 Linlin

Win condition: Leader draws extra on attack. Builds with Kyo / Stussy / Newgate / Shiki, then OP17-118 plays two Rocks Pirates (combined cost ≤9) from hand for a free board.

Effect KO: Limited. Stussy locks attackers. Laboon optional.

Key turn: 10-cost Rocks hits and dumps Linlin + Shiki (or similar).

Disrupt: Clear the character that is stopping your attackers or reducing your power. Freeze the body that is about to attack. Do not leave Stussy or Shiki active.

Your plan: Clear the board before going face. Play your closer into a body you can actually kill, not into a body that can still reduce itself or counter through.

Priority targets

  • Kyo (OP17-045) — early draw + protects characters from removal
  • Stussy (OP17-054) — locks one of your characters from attacking
  • Shiki (OP17-048) — Rush + −3000 on your characters
  • Newgate (OP17-040) — +3000 to leader on attack or defense
  • 10-cost Rocks character (already played its On Play)
  • Linlin (OP17-049) — On Play already done; low ongoing threat
Swing note: Do not swing a closer into Shiki unless you are already above his −3000 + counter line. A failed Shanks-into-Shiki swing (9000 vs 10000) leaves him rested and the threat alive. Rest or freeze Shiki first, then clear Newgate, then go face.

Purple Kaido

OP17-058 Kaido
OP17-062 Kaido
OP17-063 Kaido

Win condition: Ramps with Queen / King to reach 10 DON!! fast, then drops 12000-power Kaidos (OP17-062 blocker that also ramps, OP17-063 that removes).

Effect KO: No. Laboon not required.

Key turn: King hits (~6 DON!!) and the first 10-cost Kaido comes down.

Disrupt: Rest the Kaido that just came down. Freeze the one about to swing. Kill the rested one.

DON!! engines: Queen (rest 2 to add 1 rested), King (add 1 active + 1 rested), Yamato / Basil Hawkins (early backups), OP17-062 Kaido (recovers when DON!! is returned).

Your plan: Disrupt the DON!! engines. Keep attacking King and other bodies to force DON!! returns and keep his overall count low. Do not let him reach a clean 10 DON!! turn. Once you have your own closers, trade into the 12k bodies.

Priority targets

  • King (EB04-031)
  • OP17-062 Kaido
  • OP17-063 Kaido
Swing note: Kaidos are 12k. Expect 2-4k Counter in hand (events + small bodies + OP17-063 giving +1000 to zero-Counter cards) plus the leader's -2000. Swing at least 4k over the target (so 16k+ into a 12k) to force real investment. Rest first.

  • Any Luffy that just gained Rush or a big power buff

Black Yamato

OP16-079 Yamato

Win condition: Fills trash with searchers (Nami, Kin’emon, Robin), then plays Land of Wano characters from trash so they gain Rush from the leader. Core loop is 6-cost Yamato trashing itself to play an 8-cost Yamato with Rush.

Effect KO: Yes. Runs Ground Death (OP14-096). Keep Laboon available once trash is built.

Key turn: 6-cost Yamato hits and plays an 8-cost from trash with Rush.

Disrupt: Rest or freeze the body that just came from the trash before it swings. Clear the rested Rush body the same turn.

Your plan: Take life early. Keep the board small and active. Freeze the body that just came from the trash before it swings, then clear it the same turn. Do not swing the leader while active Wano bodies and cards in trash remain. Keep Laboon + 1 DON available once trash is built.

Priority targets

  • The body just played from trash (Rush)
  • 6-cost Yamato (OP16-098)
  • 8-cost Yamato (OP16-096 / OP16-097)
  • Kin’emon or Robin after they have already resolved
Swing note: The Rush body is the main threat. Freeze it, then clear it. Ground Death can KO your freeze or blocker; Laboon stops that.

Appendix: Rough Winning Lines

vs Black Imu

Turns 1–2: Play Kid & Killer. Develop. No real life swings yet.
Turns 3–4: Attach 1–2 DON!! to Bonney and start taking life. Rest small Celestial Dragons when possible.
Turns 5–6: Drop Hody on 7. Clear one body. Rest Ju Peter (or Mars) the moment the Elders come down. Freeze the biggest active Elder with Carrot.
Turns 6–7: Drop a 9-cost (Shanks or Zoro). Attack the rested Elder. Keep the most important one rested and race the last life.

vs Purple Enel

Early: Take life and rest the body that just received the 4 DON!!.
Mid: Freeze the 6-cost Enel. Clear the pumped body the same turn.
Late: Do not leave a 10k+ attacker active. Drop Hody or a 9-cost to finish before they can chain another dump.

vs Rocks

Early: Clear Kyo (draw + protection) and Stussy (locks your attackers).
Mid: Freeze or clear Shiki before he can −3000 you. Then Newgate so face swings stick.
Late: Do not swing a closer into Shiki unless you are already above his −3000 + counter. Clear the board first, then go face. The 10-cost Rocks On Play is already done once the extra characters are on board.

vs Ace

Rest the body about to attack (especially Newgate or a Rush body). Freeze Marco or Newgate. Clear the rested body immediately so they do not get a free second swing.

vs Sabo

Rest the cost-8+ body before it swings. Freeze Dragon or the big Sabo. Keep the high-cost body rested so the leader buff does not produce free swings.

vs Kaido

Rest the Kaido that just came down. Freeze the one about to swing. Kill the rested one. Do not let the second Kaido come down for free.

vs Boa Hancock

Rest the active 5000+ body about to swing (or the one that just triggered). Freeze the biggest one. Clear the rested body so she does not get free value from the KO.

vs Black Elbaph Luffy

Rest the Giant that just became a blocker. Freeze Loki or the big Luffy. Kill the rested Giant. Do not let them keep a wall of blockers while the cheap attackers swing.

vs Luffy & Ace

Rest the biggest active attacker. Freeze the one about to go. Clear one body per turn so they cannot chain multiple swings.

vs Pudding

Rest the big Charlotte (Linlin / Katakuri) before it swings. Freeze the one about to attack. Clear the rested body the same turn. Do not let the ramp + heal stabilize.

vs Nico Robin

Take life before the top-end arrives. Rest the highest-cost body that just came down. Freeze the biggest one. Clear the rested high-cost body immediately so the Banish + ramp does not snowball.

vs Green Shanks

Do not leave multiple bodies rested for them to lock. Rest Beckman or the 10-cost Shanks before they can fully lock you. Freeze the biggest active body. Trade aggressively once you have a closer.

vs Yellow Enel

Keep taking life even after the restore. Rest the body about to swing. Freeze the biggest one. Clear rested bodies so they cannot rebuild freely after the Life restore.
