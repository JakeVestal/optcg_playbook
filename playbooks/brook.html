Green-Black Brook (OP15-022)

Self-mill into trash, then cheat Luffys out for multi-attack finishes.

Mental model: Brook mills 4 cards a turn and does not lose until the end of the turn his deck hits 0. The real threat is 8c Nami cheating a Luffy from trash with Rush, then restanding it (or the Leader ability) for a second or third swing. Starve the trash or remove the big Luffy before it restands.

How he wins

  • Mill aggressively (Leader + other effects) to fill trash.
  • 8c Nami plays a Straw Hat Crew from trash (often a 6c or 7c Luffy) and can give Rush.
  • On the turn the deck hits 0, Leader restands a Character for an extra attack.
  • 6c Brook rests something on play; 6c Luffy has Double Attack and restands DON!!.

Tricks to watch

  • 8c Nami — the engine. She pulls Luffys from trash. Remove her or the Luffy she brings back immediately.
  • 7c Luffy (OP15-092) — scales with trash size (power, cost, Leader buff). Becomes a massive body once trash is large.
  • 6c Brook — rests a blocker or DON!! on play, then can trash itself to restand another Character.
  • Deck hits 0 is not an instant loss. He gets the whole turn, including the restand, before he loses at end of turn.

Effect K.O.?

Not the main plan. Brook’s pressure is combat after cheating bodies out and restanding. He can rest things (6c Brook) but does not typically spam effect K.O. Keep counters for the multi-swing turns; Laboon is still useful against the big Luffys if you can set them up.

How to break him

  • Remove the 8c Nami or the Luffy she cheats before it gets to restand and swing twice.
  • Pressure his life so he cannot freely mill and set up the perfect 0-deck turn.
  • The big Luffys are the win condition. Trade them or rest/freeze them so the restand does not generate free attacks.
  • He is less threatening if the trash stays small. Disrupt the mill or just race him before the engine comes online.
Remember: Kill the cheated Luffy. Don’t let the 0-deck turn become a free multi-attack.
