Brook Playbook (OP15-022)

Green/Black self-mill. Advice for when you are playing Brook against each matchup.

← Utility Cards Bonney Zoro Franky Karoo

Your plan: Mill 4 cards a turn, fill trash, then use 8c Nami to cheat Luffys from trash with Rush. On the turn the deck hits 0 you restand a Character for an extra attack. Protect the Nami → Luffy line and set up the 0-deck finish.

Black Imu

  • Elders are sticky. Your cheated Luffys can trade into them or race life.
  • Do not let Imu free-develop a wide Elder board before your Nami comes online.
  • Empty Throne cannot be destroyed by green. Ignore it.
Line: Mill, then Nami a Luffy to clear an Elder or race life. Set up the 0-deck finish.

Purple Enel

  • Rest or clear the 6c Enel so it cannot grow. Your 6c Brook can rest it.
  • Cheated Luffys can trade into the big Enel or race life.
  • Charlottes can scale — remove the small ones if free.
Line: Rest the big Enel, then Nami a Luffy to clear it or race. Set up the 0-deck turn.

Rocks D. Xebec

  • Do not let Rocks build. Prioritize Stussy, Newgate, and Captain John (must attack John if rested).
  • Your cheated Luffys can clear them. Use the 0-deck restand for an extra swing.
  • Big bodies are the problem — do not let him free-develop Newgate/Stussy.
Line: Clear Newgate/Stussy/John with cheated Luffys. Race or set up the 0-deck finish.

Purple/Yellow Robin

  • Remove the small Charlottes before Linlin. Your rest + cheated Luffys can clear them.
  • Banish is a problem — do not leave rested bodies she can punish.
  • Race before Linlin arrives, or set up the 0-deck turn to finish.
Line: Clear Charlottes. Race or set up the multi-attack finish.

Portgas D. Ace

  • Ace has early Rush. Your early mill and small bodies can trade, then Nami a Luffy to clear or race.
  • Garp is mostly a small body — ignore unless needed.
  • Marco’s On-KO is annoying. Do not overcommit into a board he can rebuild.
Line: Survive the early Rush, then Nami a Luffy to clear or race. Set up the 0-deck turn.

Red/Black Sabo

  • Sabo rests Straw Hats. Your Leader is Straw Hat, so be careful.
  • Loki is the engine — clear him with a cheated Luffy.
  • Carrot is weak against high-cost giants. If he has big bodies, focus life or Loki.
Line: Clear Loki. Watch for Straw Hat rest. Set up the 0-deck finish.

Purple Kaido

  • King is nearly unkillable by normal means — ignore him and race.
  • Your cheated Luffys can clear the other dragons or race life.
  • Set up the 0-deck turn for an extra attack.
Line: Ignore King. Clear other dragons or race with cheated Luffys + 0-deck restand.

Blue/Yellow Boa Hancock

  • Boa rests and manipulates DON!!. Your rest effects can answer her bodies.
  • Cheated Luffys can clear her enablers or race life.
  • You do not need heavy DON!! disruption; your combat is the answer.
Line: Rest and clear her key bodies with cheated Luffys. Race or set up the 0-deck finish.

Black Elbaph Luffy

  • Loki is the engine — clear him with a cheated Luffy.
  • Giants are big. Your multi-attack Luffys can trade or race.
  • Do not let him free-develop a wide giant board.
Line: Clear Loki. Trade or race the giants with cheated Luffys + 0-deck restand.

Luffy & Ace / Red-Green Luffy

  • L&A wants 6000-base bodies. Your cheated Luffys can clear them or race life.
  • Do not let him set up a wide 6000-power board that becomes 9000 on defense.
  • Race early or set up the 0-deck finish.
Line: Clear the 6000-power bodies or race with multi-attack Luffys.

Charlotte Pudding

  • Remove the small Charlottes early.
  • Race or set up the 0-deck finish before the package comes online.
Line: Clear Charlottes. Race or finish with the 0-deck turn.

Purple/Yellow Nico Robin

  • Same as PY Robin. Clear Charlottes, watch for Banish, race or finish before Linlin.
Line: Clear Charlottes. Race or set up the multi-attack finish.

Green Shanks

  • Yasopp is the enabler — clear him.
  • Big Shanks is the problem. Your cheated Luffys can trade or race.
  • Do not let him free-develop the big bodies.
Line: Clear Yasopp. Trade or race with cheated Luffys + 0-deck restand.

Yellow Enel

  • Yellow Enel wants life and triggers. Your multi-attack Luffys can race him.
  • Do not overcommit into a board he can clear with triggers.
Line: Race with cheated Luffys. Watch for triggers. Set up the 0-deck finish.

Black Yamato

  • Yamato is control/midrange. Your cheated Luffys can clear her key bodies or race.
  • Set up the 0-deck finish before she locks the board.
Line: Clear key bodies or race with multi-attack Luffys + 0-deck restand.
