Green/Black Brook Playbook (OP15-022)

Mill restand. Advice for when you are playing Brook against each matchup.

← Utility Cards Bonney Zoro Franky Karoo Rules

Your plan: You do not lose when your deck reaches 0 cards — you lose at the end of that turn. Your Activate lets you trash 4 from the top of your deck; if the deck then has 0 cards, restand up to 1 Character. The engine is milling toward empty deck so the restand becomes available, then winning on the turn the deck hits 0 (or the next). Use green/black tools to control the board while you mill. The restand is the payoff — protect the turn you empty the deck.

Black Imu

OP13-079 Imu
OP13-082 Five Elders
OP13-084 Ju Peter
OP13-091 Mars

Win condition: Flood with Celestial Dragons, then OP13-082 trashes the board and replays up to five 5000-power Elders from trash. Once trash ≥7 most Elders cannot be removed by effects.

Effect KO: No. Your tools are battle and mill, not effect KO.

Key turn: OP13-082 hits. Your empty-deck turn is the payoff.

Disrupt: Mill while you clear the value Elders and blockers. The restand on empty deck lets you swing again after a clear.

Your plan: Race early life while milling. Clear value Elders and blockers. The restand helps finish the turn you empty. Ignore The Empty Throne.

Priority targets

  • Value Elders and blockers (Ju Peter, Mars)
  • OP13-082 when it appears
Swing note: Clear an Elder, mill toward empty, restand on the empty turn for the extra swing. Empty Throne cannot be destroyed by green — ignore it.

Nasty tricks: Starts with The Empty Throne free. Plays Five Elders for 3 DON!! via the Stage. OP13-082 trashes the whole board and plays up to 5 different Elders from trash.

Purple Enel

OP15-058 Enel
OP15-118 6c Enel
OP15-061 Ohm
OP15-067 Shura

Win condition: 6-DON!! deck. Leader dumps 5 DON!! at once and attaches 4 rested DON!! to a cheap body for 10k–14k swings. Closes with sticky 6-cost Enel.

Effect KO: No.

Key turn: Leader ability goes off and a 1-cost or the 6-cost Enel receives the 4 DON!!. Your empty-deck turn is the payoff.

Disrupt: Clear the body that just received the DON!! or the 6c Enel while you mill. The restand on empty helps finish the turn.

Your plan: Take life early. Mill while you clear the pumped body or 6c Enel. Do not let Enel reach a comfortable 6+ DON!! turn with multiple big bodies. The restand helps the empty-deck turn.

Priority targets

  • The Character that just received the 4 DON!!
  • OP15-118 / OP15-060 6-cost Enel
  • Ohm, Shura
Swing note: Clear the pumped body or 6c Enel while milling. Restand on empty for the extra swing. The 6c Enel is sticky but still dies to a battle.

Nasty tricks: DON!! deck is only 6 cards. Leader adds 1 active + 4 rested and attaches the 4 to one Character for massive swings. 6-cost Enel is sticky.

OP17-040 Newgate

Win condition: Builds with Stussy / Newgate / Shiki / Captain John, then OP17-118 plays two Rocks Pirates for a free board. Captain John forces all attacks onto him when rested.

Key turn: 10-cost Rocks hits, or when Captain John rests. Your empty-deck turn is the payoff.

Your plan: Keep pressure so he cannot freely develop. Mill while you clear threats. Having your own midrange bodies is critical for trading. The restand helps the empty-deck turn.

Priority targets

  • Stussy
  • Newgate
  • Captain John (must attack if rested)
Swing note: Against a rested Captain John you have no choice — everything must attack him. Clear threats while milling. Restand on empty for the extra swing.

Nasty tricks: OP17-118 plays two Rocks Pirates for free. Captain John forces all attacks onto him when rested. Stussy locks attackers.
