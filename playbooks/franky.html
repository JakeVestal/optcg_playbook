Green-Purple Franky (OP18-021)

Stage toolbox. Stages in hand are +3000 Counter; DON!! −1 drops a Stage ≤5.

Mental model: Franky turns Stages into both free plays and +3000 counters. He drops a Stage every turn for 1 DON!!, then uses the Stage effects (draw, defense, DON!! distribution) while holding extra Stages as counters. The finish is usually a big Luffy or Franky body backed by the Stage engine. Remove the bodies that care about Stages or race before the toolbox stabilizes.

How he wins

  • DON!! −1 once per turn to play a Stage ≤5 from hand (Mini-Merry, Shark Submerge, Thousand Sunny, White Hobby Horse, etc.).
  • Extra Stages stay in hand as +3000 counters.
  • Stage effects generate value (draw + rested DON from Mini-Merry, defense from Shark Submerge, etc.).
  • Then drop 6c Franky, big Luffys, or other Water Seven bodies that benefit from the Stages.

Tricks to watch

  • Stages in hand are counters. He is not as vulnerable as a normal Stage-heavy deck. Don’t assume a Stage is dead in hand.
  • Mini-Merry — draws and adds a rested DON!!. Common early play.
  • Shark Submerge — defensive Stage that pumps Leader and Characters.
  • Only one Stage on the field at a time. He can swap them, but he cannot stack multiple Stage effects simultaneously.
  • Iceburg or Nami searchers find the Stages and Water Seven pieces.

Effect K.O.?

Not the core plan. Franky is a value/stage deck that finishes with combat. He can run some green rest effects, but he does not typically spam effect K.O. Keep counters for the Stage-backed swings; Laboon is still useful against the big bodies if the math works.

How to break him

  • Remove the Characters that care about Stages (6c Franky, big Luffys, etc.) so the Stage engine has nothing to support.
  • Pressure early. The Stage toolbox takes a turn or two to come online; race before he stabilizes.
  • Stages are powerful but he can only have one on the field. If you can force him to play a defensive Stage, he loses the offensive one.
  • He is not invincible to board pressure. Rest or freeze key attackers so the Stage value does not convert into free damage.
Remember: Stages are counters and free plays. Kill the bodies that use them, or race before the toolbox is set.
