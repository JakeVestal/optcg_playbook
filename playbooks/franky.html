Franky Playbook (OP18-021)

Green/Purple Stage toolbox. Advice for when you are playing Franky against each matchup.

← Utility Cards Bonney Zoro Brook Karoo

Your plan: DON!! −1 once per turn to play a Stage ≤5 from hand. Extra Stages stay in hand as +3000 counters. Use Stage effects (Mini-Merry draw + rested DON, Shark Submerge defense, etc.) while building Water Seven bodies (6c Franky, big Luffys). Stages are both free plays and counters — use that flexibility.

Black Imu

  • Elders are sticky. Your Stages give you counters and value while you build a body that can trade or race.
  • Do not let Imu free-develop a wide Elder board before you stabilize.
  • Empty Throne cannot be destroyed by green. Ignore it.
Line: Drop Stages for value/counters, then a body to clear an Elder or race life.

Purple Enel

  • Rest or clear the 6c Enel so it cannot grow. Your green rest effects can help.
  • Stages give you counters while you build a body that can trade the big Enel or race life.
  • Charlottes can scale — remove the small ones if free.
Line: Use Stages for counters, clear the big Enel, then race or stabilize.

Rocks D. Xebec

  • Do not let Rocks build. Prioritize Stussy, Newgate, and Captain John (must attack John if rested).
  • Stages give you counters and value while you clear his key bodies.
  • Big bodies are the problem — do not let him free-develop Newgate/Stussy.
Line: Clear Newgate/Stussy/John. Use Stages for counters while you race or trade.

Purple/Yellow Robin

  • Remove the small Charlottes before Linlin. Your rest effects can help.
  • Banish is a problem — do not leave rested bodies she can punish.
  • Stages give you counters while you race before Linlin arrives.
Line: Clear Charlottes. Use Stages for counters and race life.

Portgas D. Ace

  • Ace has early Rush. Stages in hand are +3000 counters — use them to survive the early swings.
  • Garp is mostly a small body — ignore unless needed.
  • Marco’s On-KO is annoying. Do not overcommit into a board he can rebuild.
Line: Use Stage counters to survive early Rush, then clear or race with your bodies.

Red/Black Sabo

  • Sabo rests Straw Hats. Your Leader is Straw Hat, so be careful.
  • Loki is the engine — clear him.
  • Carrot is weak against high-cost giants. If he has big bodies, focus life or Loki.
Line: Clear Loki. Use Stages for counters. Watch for Straw Hat rest.

Purple Kaido

  • King is nearly unkillable by normal means — ignore him and race.
  • Stages give you counters while you clear the other dragons or race life.
  • Do not let him free-develop the dragon package.
Line: Ignore King. Use Stages for counters and clear other dragons or race.

Blue/Yellow Boa Hancock

  • Boa rests and manipulates DON!!. Your rest effects can answer her bodies.
  • Stages give you counters while you clear her enablers or race life.
  • You do not need heavy DON!! disruption; your combat and Stage value are the answer.
Line: Clear her key bodies. Use Stages for counters and race or stabilize.

Black Elbaph Luffy

  • Loki is the engine — clear him.
  • Giants are big. Stages give you counters while you trade or race.
  • Do not let him free-develop a wide giant board.
Line: Clear Loki. Use Stages for counters and trade or race the giants.

Luffy & Ace / Red-Green Luffy

  • L&A wants 6000-base bodies. Stages give you counters while you clear them or race life.
  • Do not let him set up a wide 6000-power board that becomes 9000 on defense.
  • Race early.
Line: Clear the 6000-power bodies or race. Use Stages for counters.

Charlotte Pudding

  • Remove the small Charlottes early.
  • Stages give you counters while you race before the package comes online.
Line: Clear Charlottes. Use Stages for counters and race life.

Purple/Yellow Nico Robin

  • Same as PY Robin. Clear Charlottes, watch for Banish, use Stages for counters and race.
Line: Clear Charlottes. Race with Stage support.

Green Shanks

  • Yasopp is the enabler — clear him.
  • Big Shanks is the problem. Stages give you counters while you trade or race.
  • Do not let him free-develop the big bodies.
Line: Clear Yasopp. Use Stages for counters and trade or race the big Shanks.

Yellow Enel

  • Yellow Enel wants life and triggers. Stages give you counters while you race him.
  • Do not overcommit into a board he can clear with triggers.
Line: Race with Stage counters. Watch for triggers.

Black Yamato

  • Yamato is control/midrange. Stages give you counters while you clear her key bodies or race.
  • Do not let her lock the board.
Line: Clear key bodies or race. Use Stages for counters and value.
