Red-Blue Karoo (OP18-001)

Animal + Alabasta rush. Everything relevant attacks the turn it is played.

Mental model: Every Animal + Alabasta Character has Rush and +1000 power. He swarms the board with small-to-mid animals that attack immediately, then draws when they die. The 3c Karoo scales with power (Rush → Banish → Unblockable) once Vivi is out. Remove the enablers or trade the rush bodies before the flock snowballs.

How he wins

  • Play Animal + Alabasta Characters (Pincers, Eyelashes, Sea Cat, Super Spot-Billed Duck, 3c Karoo, Vivi, etc.). They all have Rush and +1000 from the Leader.
  • 3c Karoo + Vivi reaches 6k (Banish); one more DON!! or Let’s Go, Karoo!! makes it 7k Unblockable.
  • When an Alabasta Character is K.O.’d, he draws 1 (once per turn). Trading his bodies still feeds him cards.
  • Race life with multiple Rush attacks per turn.

Tricks to watch

  • 3c Karoo + Vivi — the combo that turns a 4k body into a Banish or Unblockable threat. Remove Vivi or the Karoo itself.
  • Rush on everything relevant. Do not assume a newly played animal is safe to ignore.
  • Draw on Alabasta K.O. means trading his bodies is not free — he replaces the card.
  • Small animals look harmless but become 5k–6k Rush bodies. Do not leave them alone if you can trade efficiently.

Effect K.O.?

Not the plan. Karoo is pure combat rush. He does not typically run heavy effect K.O. packages. Keep counters for the Rush swings; Laboon is useful if you can set up a trade into one of his midrange animals, but the volume of Rush attacks is the real problem.

How to break him

  • Remove Vivi and the 3c Karoo. Without them the flock is just a pile of small Rush bodies.
  • Trade or block the early Rush attackers so he cannot freely chip life while drawing on K.O.
  • He runs out of gas if you can clear the board repeatedly. Do not let the flock stay on the field turn after turn.
  • Race him. He is aggressive and has 4 life. Pressure his Leader before he establishes the Banish/Unblockable Karoo.
Remember: Everything has Rush. Kill the Vivi/Karoo combo and clear the flock, or race the Leader.
