Karoo Playbook (OP18-001)

Red/Blue Animal + Alabasta Rush. Advice for when you are playing Karoo against each matchup.

← Utility Cards Bonney Zoro Brook Franky Rules

Your plan: All of your Characters with both the Animal and Alabasta types gain Rush and +1000 power. Once per turn, when your Alabasta Character is K.O.'d, draw 1 card. The engine is swarming with small-to-mid Animal + Alabasta bodies that attack the turn they are played, drawing when they die to keep gas. Race life. Do not let the opponent stabilize. Look for lines into Banish/Unblockable when available. Your animals are not Straw Hats, so Sabo-style rests are less punishing.

Black Imu

OP13-079 Imu
OP13-082 Five Elders
OP13-084 Ju Peter
OP13-091 Mars

Win condition: Flood with Celestial Dragons, then OP13-082 trashes the board and replays up to five 5000-power Elders from trash. Once trash ≥7 most Elders cannot be removed by effects.

Effect KO: No. Your tools are Rush and trade, not effect KO.

Key turn: OP13-082 hits. Your Rush flock is faster early.

Disrupt: Chip life before the Elders come down. Once Elders are out they are sticky and can block. Prioritize the ones that generate value or the blockers. Trade when the math is good, otherwise keep swinging at life.

Your plan: Race early. Your Rush animals can hit life before the Elders come down. Do not overcommit into a board you cannot clear. Use the draw on K.O. to keep gas.

Priority targets

  • Value Elders and blockers once they are out
  • Life early
Swing note: Early Rush pressure, then decide whether to clear Elders or race the Leader. Your animals draw on death, so trading is not pure downside.

Nasty tricks: Starts with The Empty Throne free. Plays Five Elders for 3 DON!! via the Stage. OP13-082 trashes the whole board and plays up to 5 different Elders from trash.

Purple Enel

OP15-058 Enel
OP15-118 6c Enel
OP15-061 Ohm
OP15-067 Shura

Win condition: 6-DON!! deck. Leader dumps 5 DON!! at once and attaches 4 rested DON!! to a cheap body for 10k–14k swings. Closes with sticky 6-cost Enel.

Effect KO: No.

Key turn: Leader ability goes off and a 1-cost or the 6-cost Enel receives the 4 DON!!.

Disrupt: Your Rush animals can hit life before he stabilizes. Trade the 6c Enel or pumped body when it appears; do not let it sit and grow.

Your plan: Race early. Do not let Enel reach a comfortable 6+ DON!! turn with multiple big bodies. Use the draw on K.O. to keep pressure.

Priority targets

  • Life early
  • 6c Enel or the pumped body when it appears
Swing note: Race early. Trade the 6c Enel if the math is good. Do not leave a 10k+ attacker active.

Nasty tricks: DON!! deck is only 6 cards. Leader adds 1 active + 4 rested and attaches the 4 to one Character for massive swings. 6-cost Enel is sticky.

Rocks D. Xebec

OP17-039 Rocks
OP17-118 10c Rocks
OP17-054 Stussy
OP17-040 Newgate
OP17-044 Captain John

Win condition: Builds with Stussy / Newgate / Shiki / Captain John, then OP17-118 plays two Rocks Pirates for a free board. Captain John forces all attacks onto him when rested.

Effect KO: Limited. Stussy locks attackers.

Key turn: 10-cost Rocks hits, or when Captain John rests.

Disrupt: Do not let Rocks build. Prioritize Stussy, Newgate, and Captain John (must attack John if rested). Your Rush animals can chip life while also trading his key bodies. Use the draw on K.O. to keep gas.

Your plan: Chip and clear. Do not give him free turns to develop Newgate/Stussy. Big bodies are critical for you too — clear his threats or race before he snowballs.

Priority targets

  • Stussy
  • Newgate
  • Captain John (must attack if rested)
Swing note: Against a rested Captain John you have no choice — everything must attack him. Chip life and clear key bodies. Use the draw on death to keep pressure.

Nasty tricks: OP17-118 plays two Rocks Pirates for free. Captain John forces all attacks onto him when rested. Stussy locks attackers.
