Karoo Playbook (OP18-001)

Red/Blue Animal + Alabasta rush. Advice for when you are playing Karoo against each matchup.

← Utility Cards Bonney Zoro Brook Franky

Your plan: Play Animal + Alabasta Characters with Rush and +1000 power. Swarm with small-to-mid animals that attack immediately, draw when they die, and look for the 3c Karoo + Vivi line into Banish/Unblockable. Race life; do not let the opponent stabilize.

Black Imu

  • Imu cheats Five Elders and builds a wide board. Your Rush flock is faster early — chip life before the Elders come down.
  • Once Elders are out they are sticky and can block. Prioritize the ones that generate value or the blockers.
  • Do not overcommit into a board you cannot clear. Trade the Elders when the math is good, otherwise keep swinging at life.
Line: Early Rush pressure, then decide whether to clear Elders or race the Leader.

Purple Enel

  • Enel ramps DON!! and plays big 6c Enels that become hard to remove. Your Rush animals can hit life before he stabilizes.
  • Rest or trade the 6c Enel when it appears; do not let it sit and grow.
  • Charlottes can scale later — remove the small ones if you have a free attack, but life is usually higher priority.
Line: Race early. Do not let Enel reach a comfortable 6+ DON!! turn with multiple big bodies.

Rocks D. Xebec

  • Do not let Rocks build. Prioritize Stussy, Newgate, and Captain John (you must attack John if he is rested).
  • Your Rush animals can chip life while also trading his key bodies. Use the draw on K.O. to keep gas.
  • Big bodies are critical for you too — clear his threats or race before he snowballs.
Line: Chip and clear. Do not give him free turns to develop Newgate/Stussy.

Purple/Yellow Robin

  • Robin wants to set up Charlottes and Linlin. Remove the small Charlottes before they get buffed.
  • Banish is a problem if she has it — do not leave rested animals that she can punish.
  • Your Rush pressure can race her before the Charlotte package comes online.
Line: Kill the small Charlottes. Race life before Linlin arrives.

Portgas D. Ace

  • Ace has Rush on Whitebeard Pirates and can swing hard early. Your animals also have Rush — trade when the math is bad for him.
  • Garp is mostly a small body with an on-play. Ignore him unless the board is empty.
  • Marco’s On-KO revive is annoying. Do not overcommit into a board he can rebuild.
Line: Trade the early Rush threats. Do not let him free-swing into your life.

Red/Black Sabo

  • Sabo hangs back and rests Straw Hats. Your animals are not Straw Hats, so his rest is less punishing.
  • Loki powers up the board — remove him if he appears.
  • Carrot is weak against high-cost giants. If he has big bodies, focus life or the enablers.
Line: Your type is not Straw Hat. Use that and keep swinging at life or key bodies.

Purple Kaido

  • Kaido ramps and plays big dragons. King is nearly unkillable by normal means — ignore him and race.
  • Your Rush animals can chip life before he reaches his big turns.
  • DON!! disruption is less relevant for you; focus on clearing the attackers that can actually hit you.
Line: Ignore King. Race the Leader before the dragon package comes online.

Blue/Yellow Boa Hancock

  • Boa wants to rest and manipulate DON!!. Your animals attack the turn they are played, so her rest is less effective.
  • Clear her key bodies (especially the ones that enable her DON!! plan) and keep swinging at life.
  • You do not need Arlong-style DON!! disruption as much as a green deck does.
Line: Rush through her rest. Clear enablers and race life.

Black Elbaph Luffy

  • Elbaph builds giants. Your Rush animals can trade into them or race life.
  • Loki is the engine — remove him if he appears.
  • Do not let him free-develop a wide giant board.
Line: Kill Loki. Race or trade the giants before they snowball.

Luffy & Ace / Red-Green Luffy

  • L&A wants 6000-base Luffy/Ace bodies. Your Rush animals can trade into them or chip life.
  • Do not let him set up a wide 6000-power board that becomes 9000 on defense.
  • Race early; his defensive power is high once established.
Line: Clear the 6000-power bodies or race before the defensive wall is set.

Charlotte Pudding

  • Pudding builds into Big Mom. Remove the small Charlottes early.
  • Your Rush pressure can race before the package comes online.
Line: Kill the small Charlottes. Race life.

Purple/Yellow Nico Robin

  • Same as PY Robin. Remove Charlottes, watch for Banish, race before Linlin.
Line: Clear Charlottes. Race.

Green Shanks

  • Shanks has big bodies and can rest. Your Rush animals can trade into them or chip life.
  • Yasopp is a key enabler — remove him if he appears.
  • Do not let him free-develop the big Shanks.
Line: Clear Yasopp and the big bodies. Race life.

Yellow Enel

  • Yellow Enel is a different plan from Purple Enel. He wants life and triggers. Your Rush can race him.
  • Do not overcommit into a board he can clear with triggers.
Line: Race early. Watch for trigger clears.

Black Yamato

  • Yamato is a control/midrange plan. Your Rush animals can pressure before she stabilizes.
  • Clear key bodies and keep swinging at life.
Line: Race before she locks the board.
