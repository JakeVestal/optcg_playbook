Green Zoro Playbook (OP12-020)

Rest-tempo midrange. Advice for when you are playing Zoro against each matchup.

← Utility Cards Bonney Brook Franky Karoo

Your plan: Rest a Character (Tashigi, Kuina, or other), attack it with Leader, restand (with 3+ DON!!), then attack Leader or an 8c+ body. Use Mihawk/Law for extra tempo. Every turn with the restand is two attacks — protect that line.

Black Imu

  • Elders are usually 5k–7k base. Your restand lets you clear one and still hit life or a bigger body.
  • Prioritize the value Elders and blockers. Do not leave two high-value targets if Rayleigh is in hand.
  • Empty Throne cannot be destroyed by green. Ignore it and play around the bodies.
Line: Rest → clear an Elder → restand into life or the next threat.

Purple Enel

  • Rest the 6c Enel so your Leader can attack it, then restand into life or another body.
  • Do not let the 6c Enel sit and grow. Your rest + restand is good against it.
  • Charlottes can scale — remove the small ones if free, but life is usually higher priority once the big Enel is handled.
Line: Rest the big Enel, attack it, restand into life.

Rocks D. Xebec

  • Do not let Rocks build. Rest and clear Stussy, Newgate, and Captain John (must attack John if rested).
  • Your restand lets you clear one threat and still hit life.
  • Big bodies are the problem — rest them so the second swing can connect.
Line: Rest → clear Newgate/Stussy/John → restand into life.

Purple/Yellow Robin

  • Remove the small Charlottes before Linlin. Your rest + restand is good for clearing them.
  • Banish is a problem — do not leave rested bodies she can punish.
  • Race before Linlin arrives.
Line: Clear Charlottes with rest + restand. Race life.

Portgas D. Ace

  • Ace has early Rush. Rest and clear the early attackers, then restand into life.
  • Garp is mostly a small body — ignore unless the board is empty.
  • Marco’s On-KO is annoying. Do not overcommit into a board he can rebuild.
Line: Rest the early Rush threats, clear them, restand into life.

Red/Black Sabo

  • Sabo rests Straw Hats. Your Leader is Straw Hat, so be careful when he can rest you.
  • Loki is the engine — rest and clear him.
  • Carrot is weak against high-cost giants. If he has big bodies, focus life or Loki.
Line: Clear Loki. Watch for Straw Hat rest on your Leader.

Purple Kaido

  • King is nearly unkillable by normal means — ignore him and race.
  • Rest and clear the other dragons so they cannot free-swing.
  • Your restand is good for clearing one dragon and still hitting life.
Line: Ignore King. Rest and clear the other attackers, restand into life.

Blue/Yellow Boa Hancock

  • Boa rests and manipulates DON!!. Your rest effects can answer her bodies.
  • Clear the enablers and keep the restand line into life.
  • You do not need heavy DON!! disruption; your combat is the answer.
Line: Rest her key bodies, clear them, restand into life.

Black Elbaph Luffy

  • Loki is the engine — rest and clear him.
  • Giants are big. Your restand lets you clear one and still hit life.
  • Do not let him free-develop a wide giant board.
Line: Clear Loki. Rest and trade the giants.

Luffy & Ace / Red-Green Luffy

  • L&A wants 6000-base bodies. Your rest + restand can clear them and still hit life.
  • Do not let him set up a wide 6000-power board that becomes 9000 on defense.
  • Race early.
Line: Rest and clear the 6000-power bodies, restand into life.

Charlotte Pudding

  • Remove the small Charlottes early with rest + restand.
  • Race before the package comes online.
Line: Clear Charlottes. Race life.

Purple/Yellow Nico Robin

  • Same as PY Robin. Clear Charlottes, watch for Banish, race before Linlin.
Line: Clear Charlottes. Race.

Green Shanks

  • Yasopp is the enabler — rest and clear him.
  • Big Shanks is the problem. Your restand lets you clear one and still hit life.
  • Do not let him free-develop the big bodies.
Line: Clear Yasopp. Rest and trade the big Shanks, restand into life.

Yellow Enel

  • Yellow Enel wants life and triggers. Your restand can clear a body and still hit life.
  • Do not overcommit into a board he can clear with triggers.
Line: Rest and clear key bodies, restand into life. Watch for triggers.

Black Yamato

  • Yamato is control/midrange. Your rest + restand can clear her key bodies.
  • Race before she locks the board.
Line: Clear key bodies with rest + restand. Race life.
