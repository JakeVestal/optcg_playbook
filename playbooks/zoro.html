Green Zoro (OP12-020)

Rest-tempo midrange. Double attack with the Leader every turn it can.

← Utility Cards Bonney Brook Franky Karoo

Mental model: Zoro rests something, attacks it, restands, then attacks your Leader or a high-cost body. Every turn with 3+ DON!! on him is two attacks. Deny the first attack or the restand and the plan collapses.

How he wins

  • Attach 3 DON!!, rest a Character (Tashigi, Kuina, or other), attack it with Leader, restand, attack Leader or 8c+ body.
  • Mihawk / Law pull-back engine lets him replay rested bodies or generate extra tempo.
  • 7c OP16 Zoro and Rayleigh for board presence or clear.

Tricks to watch

  • Tashigi / Kuina — the enablers that rest and feed DON!! to the Leader. Remove them before they resolve if you can.
  • Mihawk — pulls a Character back and plays another. Stop the second play or remove Mihawk itself.
  • Rayleigh on-play — can clear two Characters at once. Don’t leave two high-value targets open if you can avoid it.
  • Leader cannot attack Characters with base cost 7 or less after the restand. He has to go for Leader or big bodies on the second swing.

Effect K.O.?

Limited. Zoro’s main removal is combat after resting. He does not spam effect K.O. the way some black decks do. Laboon/Tashigi-style on-play K.O. is not the core plan — keep counters for the double attacks.

How to break him

  • Remove the rest enablers (Tashigi, Kuina) and the pull-back engine (Mihawk/Law) early.
  • Don’t let him get free double attacks into your Leader. Block or trade the first swing when the math is bad.
  • His plan is linear and “fair.” Pressure his life or board before he snowballs the restand turns.
  • High-cost bodies (8c+) are the only Characters he can attack after restand. Use that constraint.
Remember: No free second attack. Starve the restand or punish the first swing.
