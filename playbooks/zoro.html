Green Zoro Playbook (OP12-020)

Double-attack midrange. Advice for when you are playing Zoro against each matchup.

← Utility Cards Bonney Brook Franky Karoo

Your plan: With 3+ DON!!, attack a Character with your Leader. If that battle happens, restand once. After the restand, your second attack cannot hit Characters with base cost 7 or less — it must go to life or an 8c+ body. The engine is “battle a Character → restand → finish on life or a big body.” Rest cards exist in green but are situational tools, not the core. Protect the restand turn; every successful one is two attacks.

Black Imu

OP13-079 Imu
OP13-082 Five Elders
OP13-084 Ju Peter
OP13-091 Mars

Win condition: Flood with Celestial Dragons, then OP13-082 trashes the board and replays up to five 5000-power Elders from trash. Once trash ≥7 most Elders cannot be removed by effects.

Effect KO: No. Your tools are battle, not effect KO.

Key turn: OP13-082 hits (usually at 10 DON!!). Earliest realistic is their 5th turn going second under perfect conditions.

Disrupt: Battle an Elder to trigger your restand, then the second swing goes to life or the biggest remaining body. Prioritize value Elders and blockers before they stabilize.

Your plan: Race early life so you can develop midrange bodies and start clearing rested Elders. Your restand lets you clear one threat and still hit life the same turn. Ignore The Empty Throne — green cannot destroy it.

Priority targets

  • St. Shepherd Ju Peter (OP13-084) — sets Elders to 7000
  • St. Marcus Mars (OP13-091) — On Play KO + Blocker
  • Any active Elder about to swing
  • OP13-082 when it appears
Swing note: Attack a 5k–7k Elder to restand, then the second swing goes to life or an 8c+ if one is out. Empty Throne cannot be destroyed by green — ignore it.

Nasty tricks: Starts with The Empty Throne (OP13-099) free. Plays Five Elders for 3 DON!! via the Stage. OP13-082 trashes the whole board and plays up to 5 different Elders from trash.

Purple Enel

OP15-058 Enel
OP15-118 6c Enel
OP15-061 Ohm
OP15-067 Shura

Win condition: 6-DON!! deck. Leader dumps 5 DON!! at once and attaches 4 rested DON!! to a cheap body for 10k–14k swings. Closes with sticky 6-cost Enel (immune +2000 while ≤6 DON!!).

Effect KO: No.

Key turn: Leader ability goes off and a 1-cost (Ohm/Shura) or the 6-cost Enel receives the 4 DON!!.

Disrupt: Battle the body that just received the DON!! (or the 6c Enel) to trigger restand, then the second swing finishes on life. Do not leave a 10k+ attacker active.

Your plan: Take life early. When the dump happens, battle the pumped body to restand and clear it or force counters, then the second swing hits life. The 6c Enel is sticky but still dies to a battle if you can reach it.

Priority targets

  • The Character that just received the 4 DON!!
  • OP15-118 / OP15-060 6-cost Enel
  • Ohm (OP15-061), Shura (OP15-067)
Swing note: The pumped body is often 10k–14k. Battle it to restand, then the second swing must go to life or an 8c+ body. Plan the order so the restand is useful.

Nasty tricks: DON!! deck is only 6 cards. Leader adds 1 active + 4 rested and attaches the 4 to one Character for massive swings. 6-cost Enel is sticky.

Rocks D. Xebec

OP17-039 Rocks
OP17-118 10c Rocks
OP17-054 Stussy
OP17-040 Newgate
OP17-044 Captain John

Win condition: Builds with Stussy / Newgate / Shiki / Captain John, then OP17-118 plays two Rocks Pirates (combined cost ≤9) from hand for a free board. Captain John forces all attacks onto him when rested.

Effect KO: Limited. Stussy locks attackers.

Key turn: 10-cost Rocks hits and dumps big bodies, or when Captain John rests and forces all attacks onto him.

Disrupt: Do not let him build. Battle Stussy, Newgate, or John (must attack John if rested) to trigger restand, then the second swing finishes on life.

Your plan: Keep pressure so he cannot freely develop. Your restand lets you clear one threat and still hit life. Having your own midrange bodies is critical for trading.

Priority targets

  • Stussy (OP17-054) — locks attackers
  • Newgate (OP17-040) — +3000 wall
  • Captain John (OP17-044) — must attack if rested
Swing note: Against a rested Captain John you have no choice — everything must attack him. Battle him to restand, then the second swing goes to life. Do not swing into Shiki unless you can push past his line.

Nasty tricks: OP17-118 plays two Rocks Pirates for free. Captain John forces all attacks onto him when rested. Stussy locks attackers.

Purple/Yellow Robin

OP09-062 Robin
OP17-112 Linlin
OP17-106 Smoothie
OP17-110 Perospero

Win condition: Leader attacks to Banish and ramp. Accelerates into the Big Mom / Charlotte package for Life burn and heals.

Effect KO: No.

Key turn: She ramps, then drops a high-cost Charlotte that can swing or heal.

Disrupt: Battle a Charlotte to trigger restand, then the second swing finishes on life. Do not leave Smoothie or Perospero active if they can chain.

Your plan: Race while the board is thin. Once a big Charlotte is out, battle it to restand and clear or force counters, then hit life. Banish is a problem — do not leave rested bodies she can punish.

Priority targets

  • Small Charlottes before Linlin (OP17-112)
  • Smoothie (OP17-106), Perospero (OP17-110)
Swing note: Battle a Charlotte to restand, then the second swing goes to life. Race before Linlin arrives.

Nasty tricks: Banish on Leader attack. Charlottes scale with Linlin. Heals and Life burn.
